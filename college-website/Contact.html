Government City College Details

Establishment Year: Government City College was established in 1965 as a government institution dedicated to providing quality higher education.

Affiliation: The college is affiliated with the State University and follows its academic guidelines and curriculum standards.

Accreditation: The college is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with an 'A' grade, ensuring quality education and governance.

Courses Offered: The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate courses in Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams, including B.Sc, B.Com, B.A, M.Sc, M.Com, and M.A programs.

Facilities: Our campus is equipped with modern classrooms, fully equipped laboratories, a comprehensive digital library, sports facilities, auditorium, cafeteria, and Wi-Fi connectivity throughout the campus.
