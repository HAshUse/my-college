Departments

Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science is one of the leading departments in our college, offering comprehensive education in programming, software development, database management, and artificial intelligence. Our faculty members are experienced professionals with industry expertise. We provide hands-on training through labs equipped with modern hardware and software. Students have opportunities to participate in coding competitions, hackathons, and internship programs with leading tech companies.

Head of Department: Prof. Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Last updated: 2025

Department of Mathematics

The Department of Mathematics offers rigorous training in pure and applied mathematics, fostering analytical and problem-solving skills among students. Our curriculum covers algebra, calculus, statistics, and mathematical modeling. We emphasize both theoretical understanding and practical applications of mathematical concepts. The department regularly organizes mathematics seminars and workshops to enhance student knowledge beyond the classroom.

Head of Department: Prof. Dr. Priya Sharma

Last updated: 2025

Department of Bachelor of Commerce (B.COM)

The Department of Bachelor of Commerce provides comprehensive education in accounting, business management, economics, and finance. Our program prepares students for careers in banking, finance, accounting, and business administration. We have strong ties with various financial institutions and provide internship opportunities for practical exposure. The curriculum is designed to meet industry standards and develop skills required in the corporate world.

Head of Department: Prof. Dr. Vikram Singh

Last updated: 2025
